Rock the Fall Challenge

Official Rules

The Rock the Fall Challenge is a contest of skill. Winners are chosen by judges using the criteria below. No prize is awarded by chance.

1. Sponsor

The Challenge is sponsored and run by DBAB LLC, doing business as Naked Training, 314 N 3050 E, Suite B1, St. George, UT 84790 ("Sponsor"). Questions: support@nakedprogram.com.

2. Who can enter

  • You must be at least 18 years old (or the age of majority where you live, if older) when you enroll.
  • Open worldwide to anyone who meets the age requirement, except where a paid-entry contest of skill is prohibited or restricted by law. Void where prohibited. You are responsible for making sure you are allowed to take part where you live.
  • Employees, contractors, and coaches of Sponsor, and their immediate family and household members, can take part in the Challenge but cannot win prizes.
  • Talk to your doctor before starting this or any exercise or nutrition program. You take part at your own risk.

3. Key dates

  • Enrollment opens: September 20, 2026.
  • Enrollment closes: 11:59 PM Mountain Time on October 5, 2026.
  • Challenge period: eight (8) consecutive weeks beginning October 5, 2026.
  • Photo submission deadline: 11:59 PM Mountain Time on Monday, November 30, 2026.
  • Winners announced: on or about Monday, December 7, 2026.

4. How to enter

  1. Enroll. Buy the Rock the Fall Challenge ($50) or a Naked Training Yearly Membership ($149) before enrollment closes. Members with an active Yearly Membership on October 5, 2026 are also eligible. One entry per person.
  2. Take your starting photos. During kickoff week (October 5 to October 11, 2026), take three photos: front, side, and back. In each photo, hold a handwritten sign showing the starting hashtag we announce at kickoff. Keep these photos. You will submit them at the end.
  3. Train. Follow any program in the Naked Training app for the eight weeks. Every program counts.
  4. Show up in the community. Post, check in, and support other challengers in the private Naked Training Facebook group. You will get the link to join after you enroll. When you submit your photos, include the name on your Facebook profile so we can match your activity to your entry.
  5. Submit your photos. At the end of Week 8, take matching front, side, and back photos, this time holding a handwritten sign showing the final hashtag we announce that week. Submit both sets by the deadline by emailing all six photos to support@nakedprogram.com with the subject line "Rock the Fall Entry". Include your full name and the email address on your Naked Training account. Late or incomplete entries cannot be judged.

Photos without the correct hashtag sign cannot be judged. Photos must be your own, unedited (no filters, retouching, or AI alteration), taken in similar lighting, clothing, and poses, and must show you during the dates above. Sponsor may ask for original files or other proof and may disqualify any entry it reasonably believes is altered, misdated, or not genuine.

5. How winners are chosen

A panel made up of Brooke Ence and the Naked Training coaching team will score every complete, eligible entry on:

  • Transformation (70%): visible change between starting and final photos, judged relative to where you began, not against other challengers' starting points.
  • Community participation (30%): how consistently and meaningfully you showed up in the private Facebook group during the eight weeks, including check-ins, posts, and support for other challengers.

The five (5) entries with the highest total scores win. If scores are tied, the entry with the higher community participation score wins the tie. Judges' decisions are final.

6. Prizes

  • Five (5) winners will each receive $2,000 USD. Total prize pool: $10,000 USD.
  • Prizes are paid by check, PayPal, or bank transfer, at Sponsor's choice, within 30 days after a winner is verified and has returned any required tax forms.
  • Winners are responsible for all taxes on their prize. U.S. winners must provide a completed IRS Form W-9 before payment and will receive a Form 1099. Winners outside the U.S. may be asked for equivalent tax documentation.
  • Prizes cannot be transferred or substituted.

7. Winner notification

Sponsor will contact potential winners at the email address on their Naked Training account. A potential winner who does not reply within seven (7) days, cannot be verified, or is found ineligible forfeits the prize, and the next-highest-scoring entry becomes the potential winner.

8. Bi-weekly mini-challenges

During the Challenge, Sponsor will run a mini-challenge about every two weeks (for example: hydration and sleep, daily steps, protein goals, mobility). Each mini-challenge's goal, dates, and prize will be posted in the community when it starts. Mini-challenge winners are selected by the Naked Training team based on completing the posted goal and the quality and consistency of participation. They are not drawn at random. Prizes shown on the website are examples and may vary. Mini-challenge prizes have an approximate retail value of $40 to $200 each.

9. Your photos and story

You keep ownership of your photos. By submitting them, you give Sponsor permission to use your photos, first name and last initial, results, and any testimonial you provide in Naked Training marketing, including the website, email, social media, and paid advertising, without additional payment. This applies to everyone who submits photos, whether or not they win. If you would like a photo removed from future marketing, email us and we will stop using it going forward.

10. Refunds

If you are not happy with Rock the Fall in the first 7 days after purchase, email us for a full refund. A refunded entry is no longer eligible for prizes.

11. General conditions

  • Sponsor may disqualify anyone who cheats, harasses other members, breaks these rules, or acts in an unsportsmanlike way.
  • If the Challenge cannot run as planned for reasons outside Sponsor's control, Sponsor may modify, pause, or cancel it and will judge eligible entries received up to that point where that is practical.
  • By entering, you release Sponsor and its owners, employees, coaches, and agents from any claims arising from your participation in the Challenge or use of a prize, to the fullest extent allowed by law.
  • These rules are governed by the laws of the State of Utah, and any dispute will be resolved in the state or federal courts located in Utah.
  • Information you provide is handled under our Privacy Policy.

12. Social platforms

This Challenge is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with Meta, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Apple, or Google. You release those platforms from any responsibility related to the Challenge.

13. Winners list

For the names of the winners, email support@nakedprogram.com with the subject line "Rock the Fall Winners" within 60 days after winners are announced.
